# Final Screenshot Capture Evidence

The final screenshot workflow captured 16 current public and protected routes at a 1440px desktop viewport using a self-cleaning local account and a disposable Preview Operator workspace. The screenshot workflow removed the temporary organization graph after capture.

Visual inspection of the first Today capture identified a closing-dialog overlay. The capture workflow was corrected to wait for the Walk-in dialog to become hidden and for transition idle before saving. The replacement Today image is overlay-free, shows readable Georgian labels, confirms the Walk-in entry point and server-derived unpaid payment context, and contains no real customer data.

The bundle is intended as route evidence, not as proof of completed external email delivery or of unimplemented third-party integrations.
